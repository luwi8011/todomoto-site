Electric bikes and electric dirt bikes are more and more popular around Boulder, and we offer professional maintenance, diagnostics, and repair for them.

We service a variety of electric platforms, including **Surron, Talaria, and E-Ride Pro**. Whether you're dealing with drivetrain issues, controller problems, suspension service, or general maintenance, we can help.

Unlike traditional bicycles, high-performance electric bikes combine motorcycle-style components with advanced electronics. Proper maintenance keeps them delivering maximum performance, reliability, and battery life.

Whether you ride trails around Boulder or commute around town, we'll keep your machine performing the way it should.
